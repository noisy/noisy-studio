# Mobile preview releases

## 0.2.0

- Talk-first crew view with large portraits and conversation titles.
- Tap a portrait for details or hold to address that conversation directly.
- Compact Push to talk / Auto controls and explicit Auto recipient selection.
- Reply directly from agent messages in the combined Recent feed.
- Animated cancellation targets: slide onto Cancel and release to discard; moving back keeps the recording.
- Larger talk control in conversation details, with cancellation extending to the bottom of the screen.

This preview controls microphone capture and playback on the connected desktop. Phone microphone transport is not included. Distribution uses TestFlight; it is not a public App Store release.
